module is 128x32, so keep screen width and height as constants in your code) (added) 
- Two pushbuttons (added)
- One LED with a 330 ohm resistor

        Wiring for Wokwi SIM:
            OLED (4 wires) #WIRED
                GND to any GND pin
                VCC to 3V3 (top left of the board)
                SDA to GPIO4 (right side)
                SCL to GPIO5 (right side)

            Buttons (2 wires each) # WIRED
                Button 1: one pin to GPIO6, the other to GND
                Button 2: one pin to GPIO7, the other to GND
                Wokwi buttons tie the two left pins together and the two right pins together internally. Use one left pin and one right pin, not two on the same side.
                No external resistors needed. Enable the internal pull-up in code with INPUT_PULLUP, so a pressed button reads LOW.

            Debug LED (3 connections) # WIRED
                GPIO10 to one end of the resistor
                Other end of the resistor to the LED anode (the bent leg, which Wokwi labels A when you hover)
                LED cathode to GND

            Leave unconnected
                GPIO3 is reserved for the battery ADC, so don’t use it yet
                GPIO2, GPIO8, and GPIO9 are strapping pins, so keep them free
